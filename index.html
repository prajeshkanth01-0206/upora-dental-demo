A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know before you go.

Have another question? Contact the clinic and the team can guide you.

Ask us a question ↗
How often should I schedule a dental check-up?

Many people benefit from regular check-ups every six months, but the right interval depends on your oral health. Your dentist can recommend a schedule for you.

Can I ask about treatment costs before booking?

Absolutely. You can ask about consultation fees and likely treatment costs when you contact the clinic. The final plan should be confirmed after a clinical assessment.

Do you welcome children and first-time visitors?

A family-friendly practice can help children and first-time visitors feel more comfortable. Please mention any concerns when arranging your appointment.

What should I do if I have sudden tooth pain?

Contact a dental professional promptly for advice. Severe swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, or difficulty breathing or swallowing requires urgent medical attention.
